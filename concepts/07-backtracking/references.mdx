# References: Backtracking

Books, articles and resources to go deeper into the lessons of this chapter. The textbooks are listed in order of relevance to the course.

## Textbooks

- R. Guerequeta and A. Vallecillo. *Técnicas de diseño de algoritmos*. Universidad de Málaga, 1998. Free online: [lcc.uma.es/~av/Libro](http://www.lcc.uma.es/~av/Libro/). The chapter "Vuelta atrás" has many solved problems.
- G. Brassard and P. Bratley. *Fundamentals of Algorithmics*. Prentice Hall, 1996. The chapter on exploring graphs covers backtracking.
- A. Levitin. *Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms*, 3rd ed. Pearson, 2012. Chapter 12 starts with backtracking, with the N-queens and subset sum problems.
- S. S. Skiena. *The Algorithm Design Manual*, 3rd ed. Springer, 2020. A practical chapter on combinatorial search, with advice on pruning.

## Original sources

- S. W. Golomb and L. D. Baumert. "Backtrack programming". *Journal of the ACM*, 12(4):516–524, 1965.
- D. E. Knuth. "Estimating the efficiency of backtrack programs". *Mathematics of Computation*, 29(129):121–136, 1975. How to estimate the size of a backtracking tree by random sampling.
- H. C. von Warnsdorff. *Des Rösselsprunges einfachste und allgemeinste Lösung*. Schmalkalden, 1823. The heuristic for the knight's tour.

## Online resources

- [N-queens, recursive backtracking (David Galles)](https://www.cs.usfca.edu/~galles/visualization/RecQueens.html): watch the search tree of the N-queens problem.
