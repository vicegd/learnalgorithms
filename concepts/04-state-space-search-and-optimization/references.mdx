# References: Backtracking and Branch and Bound

Books, articles and resources to go deeper into the lessons of this chapter. The textbooks are listed in order of relevance to the course.

## Course readings

These readings (in Spanish) are part of the course materials:

- *Book - vuelta atrás* and *Esquema básico - backtracking*. The first is the chapter "Vuelta atrás" of Guerequeta and Vallecillo (see below).
- *Book - ramifica y poda* and *Esquema básico - branch and bound*.
- *Agentes y tareas*: a detailed study of the task assignment problem.

## Textbooks

- R. Guerequeta and A. Vallecillo. *Técnicas de diseño de algoritmos*. Universidad de Málaga, 1998. Free online: [lcc.uma.es/~av/Libro](http://www.lcc.uma.es/~av/Libro/). Chapters on backtracking ("vuelta atrás") and branch and bound ("ramificación y poda") with many solved problems.
- G. Brassard and P. Bratley. *Fundamentals of Algorithmics*. Prentice Hall, 1996. The chapter on exploring graphs covers backtracking and branch and bound.
- A. Levitin. *Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms*, 3rd ed. Pearson, 2012. Chapter 12 starts with backtracking and branch and bound, with the N-queens, subset sum, assignment and knapsack problems.
- S. S. Skiena. *The Algorithm Design Manual*, 3rd ed. Springer, 2020. A practical chapter on combinatorial search and heuristic methods, with advice on pruning.
- S. Russell and P. Norvig. *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 4th ed. Pearson, 2020. Informed search, A\* and admissible heuristics, with the 8-puzzle as running example.

## Original sources

- S. W. Golomb and L. D. Baumert. "Backtrack programming". *Journal of the ACM*, 12(4):516–524, 1965.
- D. E. Knuth. "Estimating the efficiency of backtrack programs". *Mathematics of Computation*, 29(129):121–136, 1975. How to estimate the size of a backtracking tree by random sampling.
- A. H. Land and A. G. Doig. "An automatic method of solving discrete programming problems". *Econometrica*, 28(3):497–520, 1960. The origin of branch and bound.
- P. E. Hart, N. J. Nilsson and B. Raphael. "A formal basis for the heuristic determination of minimum cost paths". *IEEE Transactions on Systems Science and Cybernetics*, 4(2):100–107, 1968. The A\* algorithm.
- H. C. von Warnsdorff. *Des Rösselsprunges einfachste und allgemeinste Lösung*. Schmalkalden, 1823. The heuristic for the knight's tour.

## Online resources

- [N-queens, recursive backtracking (David Galles)](https://www.cs.usfca.edu/~galles/visualization/RecQueens.html): watch the search tree of the N-queens problem.
