References: Mathematical Background

Books, articles and resources to go deeper into the lessons of this appendix. The textbooks are listed in order of relevance to the course.

Textbooks​

  • R. L. Graham, D. E. Knuth and O. Patashnik. Concrete Mathematics, 2nd ed. Addison-Wesley, 1994. Sums, recurrences, binomial coefficients and harmonic numbers, written for computer scientists. The classic reference for everything in this chapter.
  • T. H. Cormen, C. E. Leiserson, R. L. Rivest and C. Stein. Introduction to Algorithms, 4th ed. MIT Press, 2022. Appendix A (summations) and the chapter on divide and conquer (substitution, recursion trees and the master theorem); loop invariants are introduced with insertion sort.
  • A. Levitin. Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms, 3rd ed. Pearson, 2012. Appendix A collects useful formulas (logarithms, combinatorics, summations) and Appendix B is a short tutorial on recurrence relations.
  • D. E. Knuth. The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 1: Fundamental Algorithms, 3rd ed. Addison-Wesley, 1997. The section on mathematical preliminaries covers induction, sums, harmonic numbers and Stirling's approximation.
  • E. Lehman, F. T. Leighton and A. R. Meyer. Mathematics for Computer Science. MIT, 2017. Free online through MIT OpenCourseWare. Induction, sums, asymptotics and recurrences, with many exercises.
