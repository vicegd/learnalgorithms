References: Parallel Algorithms

Books, articles and resources to go deeper into the lessons of this chapter. The textbooks are listed in order of relevance to the course.

Textbooks​

  • B. Goetz, T. Peierls, J. Bloch, J. Bowbeer, D. Holmes and D. Lea. Java Concurrency in Practice. Addison-Wesley, 2006. The reference on threads, shared data, race conditions and the java.util.concurrent library.
  • M. Herlihy, N. Shavit, V. Luchangco and M. Spear. The Art of Multiprocessor Programming, 2nd ed. Morgan Kaufmann, 2020. Its chapter on scheduling and work distribution explains work and span and work stealing.
  • T. H. Cormen, C. E. Leiserson, R. L. Rivest and C. Stein. Introduction to Algorithms, 4th ed. MIT Press, 2022. The chapter on parallel algorithms (fork-join parallelism, work, span, parallel Mergesort).

Original sources​

  • G. M. Amdahl. "Validity of the single processor approach to achieving large scale computing capabilities". AFIPS Spring Joint Computer Conference, 30:483–485, 1967.
  • J. L. Gustafson. "Reevaluating Amdahl's law". Communications of the ACM, 31(5):532–533, 1988.
  • A. H. Karp and H. P. Flatt. "Measuring parallel processor performance". Communications of the ACM, 33(5):539–543, 1990. The experimentally determined serial fraction.
  • R. D. Blumofe and C. E. Leiserson. "Scheduling multithreaded computations by work stealing". Journal of the ACM, 46(5):720–748, 1999.
  • D. Lea. "A Java fork/join framework". Proceedings of the ACM 2000 Conference on Java Grande, 36–43, 2000. The design of the framework used in this chapter, by its author.

Online resources​

  • ForkJoinPool (Java 21 API documentation).
  • RecursiveTask (Java 21 API documentation).
