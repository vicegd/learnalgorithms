# References: Brute Force

Books, articles and resources to go deeper into the lessons of this chapter. The textbooks are listed in order of relevance to the course.

## Textbooks

- A. Levitin. *Introduction to the Design and Analysis of Algorithms*, 3rd ed. Pearson, 2012. Chapter 3 is devoted to brute force and exhaustive search: selection and bubble sort, sequential search, string matching, closest pair, convex hull, the traveling salesman, the knapsack and the assignment problem.
- S. S. Skiena. *The Algorithm Design Manual*, 3rd ed. Springer, 2020. The chapter on combinatorial search explains how to enumerate subsets and permutations, and when exhaustive search is good enough.
- D. E. Knuth. *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 4A: Combinatorial Algorithms, Part 1*. Addison-Wesley, 2011. The definitive reference on generating all tuples, permutations and combinations efficiently.

## Related lessons

- The [backtracking](../07-backtracking/01-backtracking-scheme.mdx) chapter turns exhaustive search into a systematic search that abandons hopeless candidates early.
